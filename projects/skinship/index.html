Skinship

A community where people with skin conditions share what actually worked. In research.

The problem

Atopic dermatitis (eczema) and other chronic skin conditions affect millions of people, and most of them spend years trying products and treatments that don’t work. Doctor visits are short, and the same few prescriptions don’t help everyone. Meanwhile, the most useful knowledge (what actually calmed someone’s skin, which products made it worse) lives scattered across forums, comment sections and word of mouth.

Who it’s for

People with chronic skin conditions who are tired of guessing, and who’d trust advice from someone with the same condition more than an ad or a random review.

What Skinship does

  • Find your people: match with others who have the same condition and similar triggers
  • Share what worked (and what didn’t): products, routines and practices, with links to the products
  • See the bigger picture: how many people a product helped vs. didn’t, not just one person’s post

Designing for trust and safety

A community that shares remedies can also spread things that make skin worse. So Skinship is built to support medical care, not replace it:

  • A clear “not medical advice” note, with encouragement to check with a doctor before trying something new
  • Members can flag harmful suggestions, backed by simple moderation
  • Recommendations show how many people they worked for, so one-off claims don’t look like proof

The plan

  1. In progress User research: interviewing 8–10 people with chronic skin conditions about how they find and trust advice today
  2. Upcoming Synthesis: turning interviews into the key problems and the core features worth building
  3. Upcoming Prototyping: designing and testing the main flows in Figma
  4. Upcoming Building: a full-stack app, then launching it to a small first group of users

What’s next

Running user interviews, then sharing what I learn and early designs on LinkedIn as I build.
